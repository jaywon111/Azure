Frequently Asked Questions

1. What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is at 3:00 PM, and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are available upon request.

2. Do you allow pets?

Yes, we are a pet-friendly resort. We welcome dogs up to 50 lbs for a one-time fee of $150.

3. Is airport transfer provided?

We offer complimentary luxury sedan transfers to and from Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) for all suite guests.

4. Do you have EV charging stations?

Yes, we have four Tesla Destination Chargers and two universal Level 2 EV chargers available for our guests.

5. What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 72 hours prior to arrival. Cancellations within 72 hours will incur a fee equal to one night's stay.
